Three ways to work

Choose the model that fits where you are.

From a single engineer joining your team to a turnkey product. The model adapts to your actual situation, not the other way around.

Staff augmentation

Add engineers to your team, fast.

We embed engineers in your existing team. They use your tools, follow your process and ship alongside your people from week one.

  • One engineer or a small group
  • They work inside your team and your process
  • Scale up or down whenever you need to
  • Ideal for accelerating an existing roadmap

Dedicated team

A complete team, managed by us.

An autonomous team of developers, a tech lead and QA that works like your own, aligned with your goals and without the cost of hiring in-house.

  • Defined roles: development, technical leadership and QA
  • Tech lead included
  • We own what the team delivers
  • Built for long-term products

Custom product

Defined scope, delivered end to end.

You define the problem. We design, build and ship the complete system to production, with clear milestones and full engineering accountability.

  • End-to-end accountability
  • Scope and timeline agreed up front
  • Architecture, development and launch included
  • Milestone-based progress you can review and approve
