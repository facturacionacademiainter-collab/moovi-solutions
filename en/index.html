How we work

Four stages, no surprises along the way.

Every stage ends with something you can see and approve. If something is off, it gets fixed right then, not three months later.

Discovery

We map the real process, talk to the people who run it and define which metric needs to move. You walk away with scope, risks and an estimate.

Architecture

Data model, integrations, security and deployment plan. A clickable prototype before we build, to validate the end-to-end flow.

Build

Short cycles with demoable deliverables, automated testing and a staging environment open to your team from week one.

Operations

Production launch, monitoring, service-level agreements and knowledge transfer. We keep evolving the product or hand it over fully documented.
