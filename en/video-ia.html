How we work

From idea to final video, in four steps.

You approve each stage before we move to the next. Nothing is generated blind: the final video is exactly what you saw in the storyboard.

Brief and script

We clarify the goal, the audience and the channel. We write the script and set length, tone and formats.

Visual direction

We build the storyboard from generated keyframes: look, characters, product and camera moves.

Generation and editing

We produce every shot with Higgsfield AI, pick the best takes and edit with music, voice and color.

Delivery and versions

You get the master and every version by format and language, with one round of revisions included.
